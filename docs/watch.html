Scoring method & publishing policy ＋

buying-score-v2 uses twenty five-point pass/fail/unknown checks, retaining weights 30/25/20/10/15. Score = 5 × passes, with a fixed denominator of 100. Evidence coverage = 5 × (passes + fails)%; unknown weight stays unresolved. All numeric v2 scores are provisional evidence-based entry scores. All unknown means no numeric score. A conditional completion range assumes evaluated checks stay unchanged; it is not a confidence interval or probability of profit. Historical buying-score-v1 records retain their original definitions and are not directly comparable with v2. Read the versioned checklist and core gates. Ties are alphabetical; unavailable scores appear last.

Full reviews update all 32 assessments. Soft checks update only their own status. These are periodic advisory checks, not continuous protection or guaranteed timely delivery.
