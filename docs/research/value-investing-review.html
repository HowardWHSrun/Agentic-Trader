Revenue grows, while large AI capital spending suppresses current cash flow. Normalized earning power needs more work.

Revenue growth17.8%

Cash-flow proxy/share$7.33
Reference price$507.96

Q4 Azure and other cloud revenue grew 43%; commercial remaining obligations were $678B. Enterprise distribution and recurring software/cloud use support a durable-growth thesis; these do not guarantee capex returns.

What remains uncertain

Cash capex rose sharply. Cash FCF declined despite earnings growth. Separating maintenance from growth capex is not verified. Current FCF may understate mature earnings power, while finance leases and future commitments can understate the complete investment burden. OpenAI-adjusted EPS still includes other discrete items; do not treat it as normalized owner cash.
