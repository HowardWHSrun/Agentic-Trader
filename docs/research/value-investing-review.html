Show the model assumptions

Operating cash flow minus cash property/equipment purchases minus stock-based compensation; not a company-defined GAAP measure or full FCFE. September 17 regulatory reassessment: HOLD / DO NOT ADD

A DOJ release published September 16 reports ad-tech interoperability, publisher-data access and bidding restrictions, with six years of monitoring. These could weaken control across Google’s advertising tools and add costs. This is DOJ’s summary; the full court opinion, judgment, effective timing and stay status remain unreviewed. No quantified change to Search, Cloud or consolidated cash earnings supports a new reduction or model haircut. Revisit the call when operative terms or sustained economic damage are verified. The historical inputs below remain unchanged.

Cash-flow proxy/share$2.04

Q2 revenue was $119.8B and operating income $40.8B. TTM operating cash flow less cash capex was $53.3B. Subtracting SBC produces a $25.1B diagnostic before a complete common-equity and preferred-security bridge.

Headline EPS is not recurring earning power

Reported Q2 EPS of $9.11 includes a disclosed $6.26 equity-gain effect. Removing that effect gives $2.85; multiplying by four gives $11.40 as a simple diagnostic, not normalized annual earnings or a forecast. High capex depresses the cash proxy; leases, preferred claims and future share issuance still require treatment.
