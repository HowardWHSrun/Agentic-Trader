Before an entry

Make the setup falsifiable

01 · EVIDENCE

Setup and catalyst

Record a fresh quote, the chart session, trend and volume evidence, and a verified catalyst or event date. Explain why the move could develop within 1–5 sessions and what could prevent it.

02 · ENTRY

A condition and a price limit

State the exact entry trigger and the most you would pay. A conditional plan becomes invalid if the price runs beyond its allowed range. Do not turn a missed entry into a chase.

03 · INVALIDATION

Where the idea fails

Use actual price structure or a catalyst failure to define invalidation. Set the risk per unit first, then size within the shared risk budget and exposure caps. A tighter arbitrary stop cannot repair a weak setup.

04 · TARGET AND CLOCK

Real resistance and a time review

Identify observed resistance, its date and the realistic room to reach it. Compare reward with risk after spread and fees. Set progress checks and a dated 1–5-session deadline. A calculated 2R price does not prove that resistance or a likely return exists there.

A long-term DCF or margin-of-safety range is not an entry gate. Material earnings, legal, financing and other event risks still require review. Missing current evidence can block a trade.

After entry

Review the trade on its own clock

ProgressingContinue reviewing while the setup remains valid, price makes progress and the target still offers enough reward. Check material news before carrying overnight.
InvalidatedReview an exit when the specified price structure breaks or the catalyst fails. Do not move invalidation merely to avoid a loss.
At resistanceFollow the planned target review. Take the action supported by the current plan and price evidence; do not replace a reachable target with a larger arbitrary number.
Time expiresExit or explicitly reassess a stalled trade at its stated deadline. The fifth-session review is not an automatic extension into a long-term investment.

At most one new entry per day. This is a ceiling, never a quota. Necessary exits are never blocked by the entry limit.

Five scheduled weekday checks continue: 08:45, 10:45, 12:45, 14:45 and 15:45, America/Chicago. Quiet, non-actionable checks remain in the journal. Notifications are reserved for meaningful opportunities or required actions.

Equity indicators use completed exchange sessions; crypto indicators use complete UTC days. Crypto trades 24/7, while this monitor is neither continuous nor active on weekends. State the review deadline explicitly for each market.
